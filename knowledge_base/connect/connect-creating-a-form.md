# Creating a Form (Connect)

To create a new form in Docomotion Connect, navigate to **Forms > New**.

On the “New Form” screen, you will need to fill in the required fields.

**Important:** The **Object Name** field on the form must precisely match the **ID** of the object you defined as `isMain: true` in your JSON schema.

After filling in the necessary information, **Save the form**.
