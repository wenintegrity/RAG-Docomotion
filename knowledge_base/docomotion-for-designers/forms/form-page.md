# Form Page

*Use this page to manage the core properties and design actions of your Docomotion Form.*

**Where to find these settings:** Navigate to the **Docomotion Forms** tab in Salesforce and select the specific Form record you wish to manage.

Essential Actions

To make changes to the Form record, always click **Edit** at the top of the page first. Once your modifications are complete, click **Save** to apply them.

- **Edit:** Modify the primary Form details and metadata.
- **Delete:** Permanently remove the form. *Note: This action is irreversible.*
- **Clone:** Efficiently replicate an existing structure. This creates a new Form including all linked data models, default generation settings, and related interactive elements.
- **Preview:** View a sample output of your form. This is available only for **Active** forms and uses placeholder data for Salesforce fields.
- **Export:** Save your form locally as a file for backup or to import into a different Salesforce environment.

#### Design & Lifecycle Actions

- **Define Data Model:** Structure and organize the Salesforce data that will populate your document.
- **Open Designer:** Launch the intuitive MS Word-based editor to visually customize your layout using drag-and-drop functionality.
- **Activate:** Once your design is uploaded, click this to set the form to an ‘Active’ state. Designers can test active forms before they are live for all users.
- **Publish:** Make the finalized form accessible to ‘Generator’ users. This is the final step to move your document into production.
