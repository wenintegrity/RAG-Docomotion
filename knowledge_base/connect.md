# Docomotion Connect

[Video](https://youtu.be/XlSzgckhVKE)

Docomotion Connect expands the Docomotion application to support **non-Salesforce users**. It allows users outside of Salesforce to utilize the Docomotion document-generation engine. Docomotion Connect makes this possible by opening the engine to **any CRM or platform** via lightweight REST APIs.

The key benefits offered by Docomotion Connect include flexibility, consistent branding, and faster time-to-value.

Its capabilities include:

- Output formats: **PDF** and **DOCX**.
- External storage options: **Google Drive** and **OneDrive**, and **Callback**.
- The same designer workflow as the standard Salesforce deployment.
